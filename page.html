GAMES — LEAD STORY
VICE CITY REOPENS: GRAND THEFT AUTO VI LAUNCHES WORLDWIDE
After thirteen years, Rockstar’s Grand Theft Auto VI opens at midnight local time around the world, starting in New Zealand. Players return to Vice City as Lucia and Jason, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, priced at $79.99.
DIPLOMACY — SHENZHEN
Three leaders, one table: Trump, Xi and Putin meet
For the first time, the presidents of the United States, China and Russia sit down together — in Shenzhen, on the sidelines of the APEC summit. Nuclear arms top the agenda, but officials expect no agreement.
JUSTICE — THE HAGUE
World court rules on Myanmar genocide case
The International Court of Justice delivers its judgment today in The Gambia’s case accusing Myanmar of genocide against the Rohingya in 2017. A ruling against Myanmar would make it the first state ever found to have committed genocide.
MARKETS — NEW YORK
Anthropic’s $1.5 trillion debut
Shares begin trading today on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, after the AI lab priced the largest IPO in history — $81.3 billion at $83 a share, valuing the company at about $1.5 trillion.
SPACE — INTERSTELLAR
A signal a day away: Voyager 1 at one light-day
NASA confirms today that Voyager 1 has reached one light-day from Earth — about 16 billion miles — the farthest any human-made object has flown. A radio signal sent now takes a full day to arrive.
WAR IN UKRAINE
Crimea bridge hit again, traffic halted
The bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea was hit again overnight, and traffic is halted while damage is assessed. A strike on Tuesday brought down part of its road span into the Kerch Strait.
